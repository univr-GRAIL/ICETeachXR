next for ICETeachXR

As the project reaches its final phase, we are working on three fronts: extending the platform to more courses, evaluating potential commercial opportunities, and planning the future of the ICETeachXR platform.

Spinoff potential Research
Commercialisation

Spinoff potential

The ICE Lab research group has already produced four university spinoff companies: FACTORYAL (manufacturing process management), QUALYCO (industrial visual anomaly detection), HUMATICS (human-machine interaction), and NEEDLEEYE (AI-driven surgical robotics). The University of Verona's Liaison Office provides structured support for academic entrepreneurship and new venture creation.

We are evaluating whether the ICETeachXR platform, a working VR/AR training system with validated educational outcomes and a modular VIROO-based architecture, provides a basis for a fifth venture focused on XR-based industrial and educational training.

No specific timeline or business plan exists at this stage. The first step is assessing whether the platform, as built, has sufficient differentiation and addressable demand to justify a commercial path.

Four existing spinoffs

FACTORYAL, QUALYCO, HUMATICS, and NEEDLEEYE are all active spinoffs from the same DIMI research group at UNIVR. Their existence demonstrates that the institutional environment supports commercialisation, and that the team has the experience to evaluate this step.

UNIVR Liaison Office

The University of Verona's technology transfer office supports new venture creation from academic research, providing guidance on legal structure, IP, and business plan development.

Conference and journal publications

EU projects

The ICETeachXR work creates a documented basis for future EU research proposals on XR education, human-robot collaboration, and inclusive remote learning. We are open to new collaborators.
