About the Project

A unique educational platform for Industry 4.0/5.0

ICETeachXR bridges the gap between cutting-edge industrial environments and scalable engineering education using the power of Extended Reality.

🏭

The ICE Lab

A fully-equipped Industry 4.0/5.0 demonstrator at the University of Verona, featuring a reconfigurable production line with mobile robots, robotic arms, quality control stations, and real-time IoT connectivity.

Discover the lab
🥽

The XR Platform

A collaborative VR digital twin built on VIROO 3.0, accessible via Meta Quest 3 headset, desktop VIROO client, or Meta Quest 3 in AR/passthrough mode. Teachers create guided learning modules; students engage remotely or on-site with the same interactive environment.

See it in action
🎓

The Education

Designed for the CEIS Master's degree in Computer Engineering for Intelligent Systems, the platform removes spatial constraints and makes high-tech lab resources accessible to all students, including remote learners.

What we are building

From the physical lab to virtual reality, discover the four key dimensions of the ICETeachXR project.

🔗

ICE Digital Twin

The virtual replica of the ICE Lab: real-time machine states, bidirectional communication, and immersive navigation in Unity 6000 on Meta Quest 3 headset or desktop VIROO client.

Explore
🌐

VR Remote Learning

Students access the full virtual production line from anywhere in the world. Teacher-created tasks, gesture control, and guided learning modules, no physical presence required.

Explore
🤖

Robot Path Planning

Manipulability ellipsoids and real-time kinematics visualised on a JAKA ZU 5 robot arm. Students in the "Robotics, Vision and AI" course explore trajectory planning in immersive VR using ROS and URDF models.

Key Personnel

ICETeachXR is led by a multidisciplinary team from the Department of Engineering for Innovative Medicine (DIMI), University of Verona.

AG

Prof. Andrea Giachetti

Project Coordinator, XR & HCI

Full Professor of Computer Engineering. Director of the CEIS Master's program and leader of the INTELLIGO-GRAIL research group (XR, HCI, visual computing). Author of 170+ publications.

FF

Prof. Franco Fummi

Co-Investigator, ICE Lab Founder

Full Professor of Computer Architecture. Founder of the ICE Lab and the CEIS Master's degree. Director of the Engineering & Physics section of DIMI. Co-founder of FACTORYAL s.r.l.

FS

Prof. Francesco Setti

Co-Investigator, Computer Vision

Associate Professor. Leader of the INTELLIGO-ICV group in industrial computer vision and manufacturing automation. Co-founder of QUALYCO s.r.l. Author of 80+ papers and holder of several AI patents.
